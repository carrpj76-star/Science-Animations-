Set up the board

Key words

Focus (foci)
One of two fixed points inside an ellipse. The Sun is at one focus.
Major axis
The longest line across the ellipse. It passes through both foci.
Eccentricity
How stretched out an ellipse is. 0 is a circle. Close to 1 is very flat.
Perihelion
The point in the orbit closest to the Sun.
Aphelion
The point in the orbit farthest from the Sun.

Solving for d, step by step

  1. Start with the formula: e = d ÷ L
  2. Multiply both sides by L: e × L = d
  3. Plug in your numbers and multiply.
  4. Round to the nearest tenth.

Two different protractor setups

Learning target: We are learning why spacecraft to Mars follow a curved path and launch at certain times so that we can explain how Kepler's laws are used to plan missions.

Pick a launch date

Things to notice

  • The spacecraft leaves Earth already moving with Earth at about 30 km/s. It just needs a boost to swing out to Mars' orbit.
  • The path is half of an ellipse with the Sun at one focus, so Kepler's laws set the travel time.
  • You aim for where Mars will be when you arrive, not where it is now.
